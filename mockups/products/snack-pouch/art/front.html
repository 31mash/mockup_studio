Omprakash Vaishnav has been dedicated to Namkeen for 30 years. And it doesn’t stop there. His father started making it in 1962 and it’s been running ever since. Omprakash doesn’t want to make anything else. He doesn’t want to expand into other snack varieties. Omprakash wants to focus and continue to focus on the very Namkeen you are holding. And the recipe is quite simple for him. Use the best ingredients, no colours or artificial preservatives. Just pure natural ingredients and pure oil. Made in a small village in Jodhpur, this is quite possibly the best Namkeen ever in the sky.
